# ArtemisKit 0.5.3 release-validation record

**Release purpose:** make comparison eligibility explicit before ArtemisKit calculates a baseline
delta, so a changed workload or rubric cannot look like a normal model regression or improvement.

## Scope

- Add bounded comparison decisions: `compatible`, `qualified`, or `incomparable`.
- Withhold deltas for scenario, workload, or rubric mismatch.
- Preserve visible qualification reasons for changed target configuration or incomplete historical
  provenance rather than silently claiming comparability.
- Carry the decision through local/Supabase storage, CLI, SDK, and HTML comparison reports.

## Scope limits

Eligibility is a comparison-evidence decision, not a reproducibility guarantee, signature, legal
claim, or certification. Profiles, policy packs, language/region applicability, and cost
equivalence remain outside this increment until their contracts are introduced.
