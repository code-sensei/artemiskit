Add comparative experiment reports. `createExperimentReport` validates a saved V1 experiment result and `generateExperimentReport`, `renderExperimentReportHTML` and `renderExperimentReportMarkdown` produce technical, executive and comprehensive views offline. The validator follows core's normalization order and replays its budget ledger and concurrent admission from the recorded evidence, so forged grants, reordered events and mislabelled stops are rejected. Reports show each target's publisher, weights, deployment, generation settings and seeds; state "not applicable" for cost when every target is local and unmetered; state the concurrency level, any live approval, and the reproducibility caveat; and make no ranking or winner claim.
